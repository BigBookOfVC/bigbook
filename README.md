# The Big Book of Venture Capital

Venture knowledge alpha. Thoughtful, tactical, and actionable insights from the global startup and venture capital ecosystem.

[bigbook.vc](https://bigbook.vc)

Venture is a power-law asset class: a handful of outcomes decide the returns of the whole. Reading it well means seeing the entire chain at once, from the LPs who commit capital, through the GPs who deploy it, to the founders and operators who build with it. The Big Book of VC is built around that wholistic view of the asset class.
